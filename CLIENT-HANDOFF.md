# Client Handover Checklist

## Information the owner must supply and approve

Confirm the public showroom name and legal business identity; business model; actual dealership authorization (if any); verified full address, phone, email, hours, map pin and Google Business Profile; brands/models/variants for Scooty, Bike and Car listings, and for each listing whether its condition is NEW or PRE_OWNED and its type is SCOOTER, MOTORCYCLE, or CAR; colours, specifications, photos and usage rights; New-vehicle ex-showroom prices, Second-hand asking prices, location-specific on-road quote process, stock status; genuine offers and terms; test-ride process; finance assistance and exchange policy; service/spare-parts availability; review-platform URL; GST details only where applicable; privacy contact and retention policy; and the exact business-controlled domain. Do not ask the developer to infer any of these.

## Owner first-day checklist

Create the sole owner account from the trusted server console. Sign in only through the manually typed `/admin` path. Set the public name/contact/address/hours/maps and the real HTTPS origin. Upload rights-cleared showroom/product images. Create draft listings first, verify each field, then publish. Add offers only after dates, terms and eligibility are approved. Enable service only if the showroom provides it. Configure optional WhatsApp and review URLs only after confirming ownership and accuracy. Invite staff one at a time, share invitation URLs privately, and remove access promptly when staff leave.

## Staff workflow

1. Review new enquiries and test-ride requests daily.
2. Assign each lead to an active staff member, set a follow-up date, and use the status that reflects the actual interaction.
3. Record follow-up activity and a next action; do not include identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information in internal notes.
4. Confirm a test ride only after staff have checked the vehicle/time and contacted the customer. The customer submission is only a request.
5. Mark `VISIT_SCHEDULED` only after staff record that a visit is confirmed; mark `CONVERTED` only when staff have evidence of conversion. Dashboard totals remain operational records, not audited sales.
6. Update prices, offers, stock and vehicle details promptly. Use draft/unpublish while facts are under review. Recheck stale/low-stock alerts before relying on them.
7. Ask for honest reviews only from real customers, without fabricated content or misleading incentives. The review link is manual; the website does not auto-message customers.

## Launch approval and acceptance

The owner should verify every public label/contact/offer/price/specification, mobile layout, form acknowledgement, customer consent notice, WhatsApp click target, directions link, test-ride confirmation behavior, administrative role permissions, image usage rights, accessible keyboard labels, sitemap/canonical configuration and `/admin` absence from public navigation. Confirm the domain uses HTTPS and the business controls the domain, hosting account and source repository/ZIP. Retain the final backup and recovery test evidence.

## Optional premium care add-ons

A separately priced monthly care plan can cover dependency/security update review, backup monitoring and restoration rehearsal, uptime/form checks, stale inventory review, image optimization/storage cleanup, Search Console and lead-source reporting, and a limited hours pool for site changes. Quote integration work separately for customer-email/WhatsApp provider automation, lender or CRM integrations, online reservations/payments, multi-branch stock, or any data migration. Set explicit monthly limits, service response windows, exclusions, vendor expenses, ownership and cancellation terms.
